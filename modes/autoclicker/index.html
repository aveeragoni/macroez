Mode guide

Autoclicker

Clicks for you at any speed, up to 100 times a second, or to a rhythm you tap out. It can follow a route of spots, and stop by itself when something shows up.

Good for: Repetitive clicking of any kind, from clicker games to approving long lists, without wearing out your finger.

1

Set the speed and button

Open Autoclicker. Choose the mouse button, how many clicks per second, and how much natural variation to add. Variation makes each gap a little different, the way a person clicks.

  1. The mouse button to click.
  2. Clicks per second, up to 100.
  3. How much each gap varies.
2

Pick any mouse button

The button box opens a drawn mouse. Click Left, Right, Middle or a side button on it, or press the button on your own mouse.

3

Or tap your own rhythm

Press Tap a rhythm and tap the pad or the Space bar once for every click. It clicks in exactly that pattern, gaps and all, over and over. End with one more tap where the pattern starts again so the loop lines up.

4

Follow a route (optional)

On the Route tab, add spots with + Pick a spot and switch on Use this route. It clicks each spot in turn, a set number of times, with an optional wobble of a few pixels.

  1. The spots it clicks, in order.
  2. Add a spot by clicking where it should go.
5

Let it stop itself with Smart stop

On Smart stop, snip something to watch for, like a "Done" message. Choose what happens when it's seen: stop clicking, pause while it's there, or only click while it's there, for things like a Collect button that comes and goes.

  1. What to watch for.
  2. What happens when it's seen.
6

Press F6 to start

Watch the clicks per second, total clicks and time in Live. Under Control, choose Hold to click to click only while you hold the key, or set it to stop after a number of clicks or minutes. It never clicks MacroEZ itself or a safe zone.

  1. Where it runs, and its own start and stop keys.
  2. Control options and the live readout.
