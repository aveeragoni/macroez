Modes

Four modes, one app

Pick a mode on the left of the app. Each one keeps its own setup, and profiles save all of them.

  • Data entry
  • Filling in forms
  • Clicking through popups
  • Exporting reports
  • Testing apps

Image

Snip the buttons you click all day, like OK, Next or Accept. MacroEZ watches the screen and clicks them the moment they appear.

Classic

Press F7, do the task, press F7 again. Every key press, click and scroll is replayed exactly: same spots, same timing, on a loop.

Hybrid

Recorded steps that wait for a window or button to load before carrying on. Great for slow apps and web forms.

Reaction

Pick something to watch for, like a popup or a finished export, and a key to press. MacroEZ presses it the moment it appears.
