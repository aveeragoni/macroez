Meet the Autoclicker

More than a fast clicker. Here's what makes it different:

  • Smart stop. Snip something to watch for, like a "Done" message. When it shows up, the autoclicker stops, pauses, or only clicks while it's there.
  • Tap your rhythm. Tap a beat on the pad or the Space bar, and it clicks in exactly that pattern, gaps and all.
  • Natural variation. Every gap between clicks is a little different, the way a person clicks.
  • Route. Click a list of spots in turn, a set number of times each, with an optional wobble of a few pixels.
  • Hold to click. Clicks only while you hold its key, or press once to start and again to stop.
  • Live readout. Clicks per second, total clicks and time running. It can stop by itself after a set number of clicks or minutes.
