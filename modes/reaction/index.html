Mode guide

Reaction mode

Pick something to watch for and a key to press. The moment it appears on screen, MacroEZ presses that key for you.

Good for: Closing "export complete" popups, refreshing when a session expires, and any message that always needs the same key.

1

Snip what to watch for

Open Reaction and press F7 or + Snip to capture something that appears on screen, like a popup or a message. Add as many triggers as you like.

  1. + Snip adds a trigger.
  2. Each trigger and the key it presses.
2

Choose the key it presses

Select a trigger and set Key to press on the right. You can also choose how long the key is held, and how long to wait before pressing it again if the trigger is still there.

  1. The key it presses. Click it to choose another.
3

Click a key, or press it

The key box opens a full keyboard. Click the key, or press it on your own keyboard and MacroEZ picks it up.

4

Press F6 to start watching

MacroEZ keeps an eye on the screen and reacts within a fraction of a second. Every reaction is listed in the Activity log, with how closely it matched.

  1. What it saw and which key it pressed.
