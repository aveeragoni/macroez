Mode guide

Classic mode

Record a task once, exactly the way you do it, and MacroEZ replays it as many times as you need: the same keys, clicks and scrolls, at the same spots and the same timing.

Good for: Filling in forms, copying rows from one app to another, data entry and any routine that's the same every time.

1

Press Record and do the task

Open Classic and press F7 or Record. MacroEZ tucks itself away so you can work normally. Every key press, click and scroll is captured with its exact timing. Press F7 again when you're done.

  1. Record becomes Stop recording while it listens.
  2. A live count of what's been recorded so far.
2

Check the steps

Your recording turns into a list of steps: clicks with their spot on screen, key presses with how long they were held, scrolls, and the waits between them. Untick a step to skip it, or move it with the arrows.

  1. Every step, in order. Waits keep your original timing.
3

Change any step

Select a step to edit it on the right. Change a key, how long it's held, where a click lands (use Pick spot on screen), or how long a wait lasts. A wait can also be a random range, so it looks less robotic.

  1. The selected step's settings.
4

Pick keys from a full keyboard

Clicking any key box opens a full-size keyboard. Click the key you want, or just press it on your own keyboard. Every key works, including both Shifts, Ctrls and Alts and the whole numpad.

5

Add steps by hand and set the repeats

Use + Key, + Click, + Scroll and + Wait to add steps without recording. Under Playback, choose how many times to repeat. 0 repeats until you stop it.

  1. Add a step yourself.
  2. How many times to play it.
6

Press F6 to play it

Press F6 to start. The status shows which step and which repeat it's on. Press F6 again at any moment to stop. It ends straight away, even halfway through a long wait, and lets go of anything it was holding.

  1. Stop at any time.
  2. The step and loop it's on.
