Mode guide

Hybrid mode

Classic steps that can also wait for things. Hybrid clicks a button when it appears, or waits for a window to load before carrying on, so slow apps never throw it off.

Good for: Exporting reports, web forms that load slowly, and anything where the timing isn't the same every time.

1

Mix normal steps with image steps

Record or add steps just like Classic. Then use + Click image to snip a button it should find and click wherever it is, or + Wait for image to snip something it should wait for, like a "finished" message.

  1. The two image steps. Each one asks you to snip a picture.
  2. Your steps. Image steps sit right alongside keys, clicks and waits.
2

Set how long to wait

Select an image step to see its picture and settings. Give up after is how long it waits for the picture before moving on, so a slow day never leaves it stuck.

  1. The image step's picture, match level and time limit.
3

Press F6 to start

It plays the steps in order, pausing on image steps until the picture shows up. Each click and wait is listed in the Activity log, and F6 stops it instantly.

  1. The step it's on right now.
  2. The steps it's working through.
