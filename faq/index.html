FAQ

Questions

Something not covered here? Check the notes for each version on the Versions page.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Windows shows that for apps from small developers that haven't bought a code-signing certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only download MacroEZ from this site.

My antivirus flagged it.

Apps that press keys and click for you, packed into a single file, sometimes get flagged by mistake. If you're unsure, don't run it.

Is it OK to use at work, or in games?

Usually, but check first. Some workplaces don't allow installing your own tools, so ask IT if you're unsure. Many online games ban automation and can punish accounts for it. You use MacroEZ at your own risk.

Where are my macros saved?

Next to MacroEZ.exe, in config.json and the images folder. Keep the .exe in its own folder and back up that folder to keep your macros.

How do I update to a new version?

Close MacroEZ, download the new version, and put it in the same folder in place of the old MacroEZ.exe. Your macros and settings stay. See what changed on the Versions page.

The hotkeys don't do anything.

On many laptops the F-keys control brightness and volume unless you hold Fn. Open Settings in MacroEZ, press a key and watch "Last key seen", or pick a different key or a mouse side button.
