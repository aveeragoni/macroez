Features

Built to just work

Everything here is in the free download. No accounts, no subscriptions.

  • Record and replay. Key holds, combos, mouse drags and scrolling are kept, and playback timing is accurate to about a millisecond.
  • Scroll steps. Scroll up, down, left or right, wherever the mouse is or at a set spot, spread over as long as you like.
  • Fast detection. Checks a full screen of images in a fraction of a second. Star the important ones to check them first.
  • Works on changing backgrounds. Merge a few snips of the same button and MacroEZ learns its outline, so whatever is behind it doesn't matter.
  • Backup detection. If the normal check misses, a second method looks for the button's shape.
  • Safe zones. Draw areas it must never click, like a close button or a Send button.
  • Profiles. Save as many macros as you like and switch between them.
  • Hotkeys you choose. Start, stop, snip and test from inside any app, with any F-key, numpad key or mouse side button.
  • Only runs where you want. Each mode can be tied to one app, and it waits whenever that app isn't in front.
  • One file. No installer and no account. Your macros are saved in the same folder.
