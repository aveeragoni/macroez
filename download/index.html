MacroEZ for Mac

For macOS 11 or newer. Pick the one for your Mac's chip ( → About This Mac).

Download for Apple chip (M1 and newer) Download for Intel

Unzip it and drag MacroEZ into Applications. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Then let MacroEZ use Accessibility, Input Monitoring and Screen Recording when it asks, so it can click, watch your hotkeys and see the screen.

Getting started

  1. 1

    Give it a folder

    Move MacroEZ.exe into its own folder, like Documents\MacroEZ. Your macros are saved next to it.

  2. 2

    Open it

    If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Why?

  3. 3

    Pick a mode

    Choose a mode on the left, set it up, then press F6 to start and stop. See the modes

Updating? Replace the old MacroEZ.exe with the new one in the same folder. Your macros stay.
